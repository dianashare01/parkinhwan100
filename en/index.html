“ Open the red door, 1926 ”

— Park In-hwan (1926–1956) · born in Inje, Gangwon

Born in Inje, Gangwon, in 1926, poet Park In-hwan opened the bookshop Marie Seosa in Jongno in 1945. Poets, painters and writers came and went; the shop closed after three years, but his poems became songs and films and are still read today. On the centenary of his birth, we open the bookshop’s red door again. This is not an exhibition for reading poems — it is one for walking through a day in the poet’s life.

Seven Words — How to Take Part
